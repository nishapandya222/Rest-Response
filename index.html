Comfort Crochet Research

About the Project

Comfort Crochet Research is an independent research initiative exploring wheather learning to crochet can reduce stress and anxiety. Participants will complete a survey before and after a guided crochet activity, and the results will be compared to evaluate any changes in their emotional well-being.
