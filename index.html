Research Overview

Research Question

Can creating handmade crocheted comfort items improve stress levels, emotional well-being, and perceived anxiety among adolescents?

Methodology

  • Recruit volunteer participants
  • Complete an anonymous pre-study survey
  • Create a crochet comfort item
  • Complete an anonymous post-study survey
  • Analyze changes in participant responses
