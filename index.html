Comfort Crochet Research

Exploring whether making and receiving handmade crocheted comfort items can reduce stress, support emotional well-being, and promote positive mental health among teenagers through research.

Learn More

Research Question

Can crocheting handmade comfort items positively impact the mental health and emotional well-being of teenagers?

Purpose

This project explores whether crochet can serve as a creative coping strategy by encouraging relaxation, mindfulness, and emotional comfort for teenagers.

Research Method

The project uses anonymous surveys to investigate how crocheting and receiving handmade comfort items may affect stress, anxiety, and emotional well-being.

By Nisha Pandya
