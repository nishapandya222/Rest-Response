The Rest Response Project

Exploring how everyday factors affect the health and physiology of high school students.

Research Question

What is the relationship between sleep habits, stress levels, and physical well-being among high school students?

About the Project

The Rest Response Project is a student-led research project focused on understanding how everyday factors such as sleep and stress relate to the physical health and well-being of high school students.

Through research, anonymous data collection, and analysis, this project aims to better understand patterns in student health and share findings that may help students become more aware of how their daily habits affect their bodies.
