About Me

Hi, I'm Nisha!

I am a high school student with a strong interest in medicine, human physiology, and health research.

I created The Rest Response Project because I wanted to explore how factors that students experience every day, such as sleep and stress, may relate to their physical and mental health.

I am especially interested in learning more about the human body, the nervous system, and medicine. In the future, I hope to pursue the pre-med track in college and eventually explore a career in anesthesiology.

Through this project, I hope to gain experience with research, data analysis, scientific writing, and health education while studying issues that directly affect students.
